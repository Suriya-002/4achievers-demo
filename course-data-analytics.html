Curriculum

A 9-Module Path From Spreadsheets to Dashboards

Structured, project-backed modules designed with input from working data analysts.

Lookup & reference functions (VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH), logical & text functions, pivot tables & slicers, conditional formatting, data cleaning techniques, what-if analysis, and building your first interactive Excel dashboard.

Relational database basics, SELECT queries, filtering & sorting, JOINs across multiple tables, GROUP BY & aggregate functions, subqueries, window functions, and writing analyst-grade queries to answer business questions.

Descriptive statistics, measures of central tendency & dispersion, probability fundamentals, distributions, correlation vs causation, hypothesis testing and confidence intervals — explained in plain business language.

Python basics for non-coders, working with Pandas DataFrames, data cleaning & wrangling, grouping & merging, exploratory data analysis, and plotting with Matplotlib & Seaborn.

Connecting & transforming data with Power Query, building the data model, writing DAX measures, creating interactive visuals, drill-through & filters, and publishing reports for stakeholders.

Tableau interface & data connections, calculated fields, parameters, maps & charts, building interactive worksheets, and assembling polished, executive-ready dashboards.

Dashboard design principles, choosing the right chart, defining and tracking KPIs, layout & colour best practices, performance optimisation, and building dashboards that people actually use.

Framing insights for decision-makers, structuring a data narrative, presenting findings, building a case with evidence, and communicating recommendations clearly to non-technical audiences.

An end-to-end capstone combining SQL, Python and BI tools, portfolio polishing, resume & LinkedIn optimisation, SQL & analytics interview question drills, and mock interviews with feedback.
